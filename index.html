Eun Jung

AI security researcher · AI engineer

I received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Sungkyunkwan University in 2026, where I was advised by Professor Hyoungshick Kim. I also earned a B.S. in Data Technology (Software Convergence) from Myongji University.

I am currently an intern at Smilegate AI Center, Behavior AI Team, working on LLM-based AI NPC technology development. Previously, I served as a Visiting Researcher at the University of Maryland, College Park, advised by Professor Yonghwi Kwon at the University of Maryland Cybersecurity Center.

Email LinkedIn GitHub

Research Interest

LLM Evaluation and Reliability · Trustworthy AI · AI Agents

My research focuses on LLM evaluation and reliability, trustworthy AI and AI agents. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent agents that can interact naturally with users while maintaining reliability, safety, and consistent behavior. Through my work on LLM security, robustness evaluation, and AI NPC development, I aim to build AI systems that are both effective and trustworthy in practical applications.
